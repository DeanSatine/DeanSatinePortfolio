About the Project

Cybernetic Strategy is an elite, tactical Sci-Fi Auto Battler where you must craft a legion of Robots within the Dominion in order to be The Strongest.

Calculate the most superior strategies to triumph over all as you utilize the power of Magic, Armoury or Brute force to win!
